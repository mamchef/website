About MamChef

One evening a home-cooked dinner saved the day — and we realised how many great cooks in our neighbourhood had no easy way to sell their food. MamChef started with a simple question: what if finding fresh, homemade meals was as easy as calling a taxi?

Today MamChef helps home cooks earn reliably and gives customers a better, affordable alternative to takeout.

Frequently asked questions

For Customers

All meals are prepared by independent home cooks (chefs) in Vilnius, who are registered with the State Food and Veterinary Service (VMVT) and meet hygiene regulations.
Payments are made securely through MamChef using card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay. All transactions are processed through our secure payment system.
Yes — some chefs offer delivery ("chef delivery"), while others provide pickup. You can choose your preferred option during checkout.
Each meal listing includes detailed ingredient information and allergen warnings. You can also filter meals by dietary preferences like vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and more.
You can request support directly via the app or website. Refunds and disputes are handled fairly between you and the chef, with MamChef support team helping when needed.

For Home Cooks (Chefs)

Yes — all chefs must register their activity with VMI (for taxes) and VMVT (for food safety approval). MamChef provides a step-by-step guide to help you.
There are no upfront fees. MamChef charges a small commission only when you sell a meal.
Payouts are handled through Stripe Connect. Chefs receive their earnings every two weeks, directly to their bank account, minus MamChef's commission.
Once approved, you can upload meals, set portion sizes, add photos, and choose your own pricing and pickup/delivery times.
We provide onboarding guidance (VMVT & VMI), marketing exposure on our platform, and secure payment processing.
