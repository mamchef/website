How it works

A day in your kitchen

Four moments, all in one app.

An order arrives

Your phone lets you know. You have 90 minutes to answer, and we remind you before time runs out.

Accept, and the card is charged

When a customer orders, their card is only held. It is charged the moment you accept. If you say no, nothing is taken.

Cook, then hand over or deliver

Customers collect from you, or you deliver, whichever you offer. Once you accept, you also see their phone number.

Get paid via Stripe

Your share goes to your bank through Stripe, including the delivery fee you keep.

FAQ

Questions from cooks

Something else? Write to support@mamchef.com.

Do I need a licence?

Yes. Cooks sell food legally in Lithuania, so you register with VMVT (food safety) and VMI (tax). We guide you through both inside the app. Not registered yet? Start the sign-up and follow the steps.

What does it cost?

Nothing to join. MamChef takes a small commission only on what you sell.

How do I get paid?

Through Stripe, straight to your bank account. You connect it once during sign-up.

When is the customer charged?

Their card is held when they order and charged when you accept. Decline, and nothing is taken.

Can I set my own prices and hours?

Yes. Your menu, prices, opening hours, preparation time and portions per day are yours.

Pickup or delivery?

You choose pickup, delivery or both. If you deliver, the delivery fee goes to you.

How long does approval take?

Our team reviews every cook by hand, usually within a few business days. Until then you can build your menu.

Is it safe to have customers at my home?

You decide. Offer delivery only, and your address is never shown to customers. If you offer pickup, your kitchen page shows only your street; the full address goes to a customer after you accept their pickup order. Once you accept, you also see their phone number.

What if I cannot make an order?

Just decline. Nothing is charged to the customer. You have 90 minutes to answer.

I live outside Vilnius. Can I join?

We are starting in Vilnius. Write to us at support@mamchef.com and tell us your city.
