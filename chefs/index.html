How it works for chefs

Four steps from sign-up to your first payout.

  1. 1

    Sign up and get verified

    Create your account, fill in your personal details and VMVT registration number, upload one supporting document, then sign the partner agreement online. We guide you through the VMVT and VMI registration step by step.

  2. 2

    Get approved and connect payouts

    Our team reviews your application. Once approved, you connect your bank account so your earnings have somewhere to go.

  3. 3

    Set up your kitchen

    Set your opening hours, choose pickup, delivery or both, and add your meals with photos, portion sizes and your own prices.

  4. 4

    Cook, hand over, get paid

    Accept orders as they come in, prepare the food and hand it over for pickup or deliver it. Your earnings are paid directly to your bank account.

What you need to start

A short checklist before you sign up.

  • A kitchen in Lithuania

    You cook from home in Lithuania and register your activity with VMVT for food safety and with VMI for taxes. Not registered yet? We give you a step-by-step guide.

  • Your personal details

    Your personal code, a Lithuanian phone number and your home address for the partner agreement.

  • VMVT number and one document

    Your VMVT registration number and one supporting document, uploaded as a PDF or a photo.

  • A bank account

    Payouts go straight to your bank account. You connect it once, after approval.
